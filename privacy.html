Render Board privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Render Board is an internal review tool of a VFX studio team. It runs on the studio's own computer inside the office network and is used only by the studio's employees. "Sign in with Google" lets them open it with their company Google account instead of a separate password.

What we receive from Google

Only what the sign-in asks for: your email address, whether Google has verified it, and your name (scopes openid, email, profile). Render Board does not ask for and cannot read your mail, files, calendar or any other Google data.

How it is used

The email address identifies your Render Board account; only addresses of the studio's company domain are let in. The name becomes the display name of a new account. Nothing else is done with this data.

Where it is kept, and who sees it

Your email and name are stored in the board's database on the studio's computer, as part of your account, and are visible to your colleagues on the board. They are not sold, shared with or sent to anyone else, and not used for advertising or analytics. Google access tokens are used once to read your identity and are not stored.

This page (on GitHub Pages) only passes the sign-in result from Google on to the board. It stores nothing and loads no trackers.

Removing your data

Ask a Render Board administrator to delete your account; your email and name are removed with it. You can also remove the board's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: the Render Board administrator of your studio team.
